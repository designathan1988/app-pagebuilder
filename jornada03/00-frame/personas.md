# Personas (equal weight)

The study plays three professionals who would buy a desktop page builder. Each comes with a client job to deliver, the
tools they use today, what they value and what makes them leave. During the sessions the persona sees only the
target's pictures and brief (never its code), knows the app only through what the app itself shows (no source, no
manifest, no spec), and stops a task at its time limit or after the same dead end three times.

## Marina — freelance web designer

- 31, works alone, 3–4 client sites a month (cafés, clinics, studios). Designs in Figma, builds in Framer or Webflow
  today; pays for both and resents the hosting lock-in and the per-site plans.
- Job: "deliver the client's landing page faithful to my layout, on every screen size, in one day".
- Values: visual fidelity (type, spacing, colour exactly as designed), images and her own fonts, responsive she can
  see and tune, a preview she can send the client, speed.
- Leaves when: she cannot place something where she sees it, spacing fights her, the phone layout breaks and she
  cannot tell why, the result does not look like her design.
- Language: pt-BR. Skill with code: reads CSS, does not write it.

## Diego — front-end developer at an agency

- 27, React/Next at work; receives designs and hand-codes them. Would use a visual builder only if the output is code
  he would accept in a pull request.
- Job: "build the page visually and get code I would accept in a review".
- Values: semantic HTML, a small readable stylesheet, classes and variables he can name, components, importing an
  existing page, the keyboard, no lock-in.
- Leaves when: the export is div soup or inline styles, names are generated garbage, there is no way to reuse, the
  mouse is mandatory.
- Language: en. Skill with code: high.

## Carla — agency production

- 38, keeps ten similar small sites alive for a franchise network; designers hand her templates, clients ask for
  changes every week.
- Job: "keep ten similar sites up to date: change the brand everywhere, make pages from other pages, fill catalogues
  from data".
- Values: reuse, changes made once and applied everywhere, data (spreadsheets) in, consistency, never losing work.
- Leaves when: she must repeat the same edit on every page, a change breaks another page, work is lost.
- Language: pt-BR. Skill with code: none; spreadsheets: high.
